Data Storage and Collection

Tic Tac Toe is designed as an offline game that operates without requiring user accounts or collecting personal information. Here's what you need to know about your data:

What Data We Store

  • Game Preferences: Settings and preferences saved locally on your device
  • Game Statistics: Your scores and game history stored locally
  • Temporary Game State: Current game progress during active gameplay

What Data We DON'T Collect

  • No user accounts or registration data
  • No email addresses, names, or personal identifiers
  • No cloud storage or remote data synchronization
  • No analytics or tracking data
  • No location or device identifiers

Data Deletion Process

Since Tic Tac Toe doesn't collect or store personal information on external servers, data deletion is straightforward and entirely within your control.

How to Delete Your Data

Method 1: Clear App Data (Recommended)

  • Go to your device's Settings
  • Navigate to Apps or Application Manager
  • Find and select "Tic Tac Toe"
  • Tap on "Storage" or "Storage & cache"
  • Tap "Clear data" or "Clear storage"
  • Confirm the action

Method 2: Uninstall the App

  • Long-press the Tic Tac Toe app icon
  • Select "Uninstall"
  • Confirm the uninstallation
  • Note: This will remove the app and all associated data

What Happens After Deletion

When you delete your app data or uninstall the application:

  • All game statistics will be permanently deleted
  • Game preferences will be reset to default settings
  • Saved game states will be lost
  • No data remains on your device related to the app
  • No data exists on our servers (we don't store any)

Data Recovery

⚠️ Important: Once data is deleted through the methods above, it cannot be recovered. All game progress, statistics, and preferences will be permanently lost.

No Account Deletion Required

Tic Tac Toe does not use user accounts, so there's no account deletion process. Your data exists only on your device and is completely under your control.
